CONNECTED

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

ConnectED ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that helps you write personalized LinkedIn connection requests. This policy explains what data the extension handles and what happens to it.

What the extension reads

Only when you click the extension on a LinkedIn profile, ConnectED reads the visible information on that profile page. This includes the person's name, headline, current role, experience and education. It does not run on any other website and does not read pages in the background.

How that data is used

Profile information is used for one purpose only: drafting a connection note for you. To generate the note, the profile text and your selected networking goal are sent to [AI PROVIDER] for processing. The generated note is returned to the extension and shown to you. ConnectED does not keep a copy of the profile data after the note is generated.

What is stored

Your settings, such as your networking goal and preferences, are saved locally in your browser using Chrome storage. They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not use or transfer data for any purpose other than drafting your connection note.
  • We do not use data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.
  • We do not track your browsing history, clicks or activity.
  • We do not send messages or take actions on LinkedIn for you. You review and send every note yourself.

Third parties

Profile text is shared with [AI PROVIDER] solely to generate your note, under their API terms. No other third parties receive your data.

Your control

You can clear saved settings at any time by removing the extension from Chrome, which deletes all of its locally stored data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
